## Rules

1. Secrets are read by `src/altdata/secrets.py` from the environment or from `.env`; they are never printed, logged or committed. The ENTSO-E token travels in the request URL, so URLs and exceptions are masked before display.
2. Raw downloads go to `data/`, which is ignored by git; the SHA-256 of the raw EIA files is recorded in `results/data_versions.json`, and every result file carries the pre-registration hash.
3. Availability timestamps, not event timestamps, define what is known at a decision time.

## Tank attributes in this repository (what the availability indicator lacks)

- Registry (`results/tank_registry.csv`, OpenStreetMap): `osm_id`, position, `diameter_m`, `area_m2` for 333 tanks; `content` on 2, `operator` on none, `roof` on none; no height, capacity or product.
- Per tank and date (`results/v2/BS1/fits.csv.gz`): status, depth with interval, fit parameters, `clear` flag, sun angles; an estimate (or a full-or-fixed bound) on a median 62% of dates per tank; 4 of 234 tanks are full-or-fixed on at least half of their dates.
- Per week (`results/v2/BS2/weekly_table.csv`): a scene is selected in 159 of 411 weeks, a volume change exists in 62 (median valid area share 0.81); median gap between scene and the Friday stock reference 28 h (90th percentile 43 h; measured against the frozen protocol's 07:00 CT, while the EIA reference is 07:00 ET: each gap shifts by one hour and no selected scene changes).
- The BS0 indicator is a scene-level yes/no. It does not say which tanks are covered each week, nor operator, roof type, capacity, content or membership in the EIA perimeter, nor how the registry changed over time: these need by-tank vendor data or a verified registry.
