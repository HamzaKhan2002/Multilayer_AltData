## 2026-10-25, before `scripts/a_price_proxy.py` is run (no AR, AH or AC outcome statistic exists yet)

No deviation from the pre-registration. Facts that exist before this entry: the B0 event list and its exclusions (`results/v2/deviations_v2.md`), the clock rule AM1 and its checks, the 10:30 ET profile of the EIA days (`results/v2/B0/sanity_clock.json`). The structural clock check (bars in hour 17 over bars in hour 16 on API days) is a validity gate and may be printed by `--dry` before the run; no return is read there.

Implementation choices that the pre-registration leaves open, fixed now:

1. **Event list.** `b0_transmission.candidate_events()` (registry rows without the data-only note, dated by the archive folder, 2013-08-01..2023-12-31 and 2026-06-01..2026-10-24), with its three flags; the API day is the event day minus one calendar day.
2. **Arrays.** Closes at the 120 minute starts from 16:00 on the API day (index = minute of the hour for hour 16, +60 for hour 17) and from 09:00 on the event day. P(t) is the close of the bar starting one minute before t: P(16:30) is index 29 and P(17:00) index 59 of the API-day array; P(09:30) is index 29, P(10:29) index 88, P(10:30) index 89 and P(10:36) index 95 of the event-day array.
3. **Exclusions, in order, each event counted under its first reason.** holiday_shifted, data_only_same_day, other_release_time (flags of the B0 list); missing_bar (any of the six closes absent); nonpositive_price (any of the six closes at or below zero, since returns are logarithmic); structural_clock_year (a year whose ratio of hour-17 to hour-16 bars on API days exceeds 0.25; the ratio is computed on events that passed the first three exclusions and have a bar in hour 16).
4. **Blocks.** By calendar date of the event day: overlap 2013-08-01..2019-08-31, post 2019-09-01..2023-12-31, recent 2026-06-01..2026-10-24. The verdict uses overlap only; the other two are descriptive.
5. **Statistics.** Pearson r with the Fisher-z 95% interval (sd 1/sqrt(n-3)); slope of r_pre on r_api by OLS with HC1 errors and normal 95% intervals; Spearman rank correlation; R2. Sign rule: G = sign(r_api) x (P(10:29) - P(09:30)) in cents per barrel, no cost deducted; the mean is compared with 1.5 and 2.5 cents (assumed round-trip cost) only as a yes/no. No outlier rule and no winsorisation; the five largest |r_pre| are listed descriptively.
6. **Horizon standard deviations (AH).** Sample standard deviation (ddof 1) and mean absolute value of the four moves of the pre-registration over the included events of overlap and post pooled (also reported per block).
7. **Descriptive clock profile (AC).** Per year, over events that passed the first three exclusions and have a bar in hour 16: mean absolute one-minute change of the minute starting 16:30 on the API day and of the 15 minutes before it, and the same for 10:30 on the event day. Reported, not a gate.
8. **Single run.** `scripts/a_price_proxy.py` refuses to run if `results/v3/a_price_proxy.json` exists; no statistic of AR or AH is printed before that file is written; the trial line goes to `results/v3/trial_log_v3.csv`. `--selftest` exercises the statistics on synthetic data only.

## 2026-10-25, amendment AM-A-1, written after the dry run printed the structural clock check and before any outcome statistic

Known at this point: the dry run (no return read, nothing written) printed, per year, bars starting in New York hour 17 over bars in hour 16 on API days: 2013 0.275 (17 events), 2014 0.248, 2015 0.191, 2016 0.006, 2017 0.006, 2018 0.011, 2019 to 2023 and 2026 0.000. With the rule as frozen (exclude a year above 0.25), 2013 would be excluded and 2014 would pass by a margin of 0.002.

Reason: the frozen rule assumed a halt at 17:00 ET in every year. Before 21 September 2015 the CL daily halt began at 17:15 ET (CME schedule change; reference [11] of `I1_Transmission_scientifique_2026-10-24.md`), so bars between 17:00 and 17:14 are expected in 2013, 2014 and part of 2015: the ratios above are what a correct clock gives. The check was badly specified for those years; it does not show a wrong clock.

Amended rule (validity only, no outcome): the structural check counts bars starting 17:15..17:59 (absent under both halt schedules) over bars in hour 16, and a year is excluded as `structural_clock_year` above 0.25 as before. The count of bars 17:00..17:14 is reported beside it, descriptively, to show the change of September 2015. Everything else is as frozen. The frozen file `prereg/tests_v3.json` is unchanged (hash verified at every run).

## 2026-10-26, time zone of the Friday stock reference (documentation correction; frozen v2 unchanged)

The frozen v2 protocol (`prereg/tests_v2.json`, `eia_reference_time`) and the BS0 and BS2 code take the EIA stock reference as Friday 07:00 America/Chicago. The EIA survey instructions state it as 7 a.m. Eastern Time on Friday (Forms EIA-800, EIA-803 and EIA-804: "Report quantities as of 7 a.m. Eastern Time Friday"; EIA-803 is the weekly crude oil stocks report). The Weekly Petroleum Status Report explanatory notes (Appendix B) give the report period as 7:01 a.m. Friday to 7:00 a.m. the following Friday without naming a zone. Post hoc check, not a test, nothing stored is replaced: `scripts/et_reference_sensitivity.py` recomputes the BS0 availability and the BS2 hub test in scratch folders with the Eastern reference (output `results/v3/et_reference_sensitivity.json`). Result: 0 of 410 weeks change their selected acquisition; every BS0 share and the BS2 results (primary n 19, r 0.1606, verdict inconclusive; all seasons n 57, r 0.1299) are identical. The documents now state the reference as 07:00 ET and mention that the frozen protocol used Central time. Median and 90th percentile gaps quoted in the documents (28 h and 43 h) were measured against the frozen reference; with the Eastern reference each gap shifts by one hour.
